#+title: YFCC-10M Four-Method Filtered Search Sweep

* Scope

This report compares exactly four SINGLE_CTA methods over the same 64 supported =(L, W,
max_iterations)= cells: default CAGRA and FAVOR retention-safe traversal, each with and without the
bounded passing-result accumulator.  The external benchmark batch contains 10,000 queries
and uses one repetition after warmup.  Every method uses =max_queries=512= internally.  Both FAVOR
curves include per-query selectivity sampling end to end; both default methods perform no selectivity
estimation.

The two =W=4= cells at =max_iterations in {4176,7569}= are excluded for every method because
their required visited set exceeds CAGRA's hard 1M-slot hash table at the default 0.5 fill limit.
All included rows have zero filter violations and zero invalid-sentinel errors.

* Paired default-accumulator effect

Across the 64 exactly matched cells, the accumulator improves recall in 64 cells.  Its
mean recall delta is +0.236447; its mean paired QPS delta is
-25.93% relative to default CAGRA.

* Matched four-method effects

Adding the accumulator to FAVOR improves recall in 63 of 64 cells and
QPS in 15; its mean recall delta is
+0.059931 and mean QPS delta is -11.56%.

Without accumulators, FAVOR beats default recall in 64 cells and QPS in
0; its mean deltas are +0.171985 recall and
-30.05% QPS.  With accumulators enabled on both sides, FAVOR beats default
recall in 9 cells and QPS in
0; its mean deltas are
-0.004532 recall and
-19.19% QPS.
